---
name: Conditional follow-ups
description: How "Nếu ... thì sao?" questions may relate to the previous answer without borrowing evidence.
---

A question that names its own condition and leaves the consequence open ("Nếu em phải học lại một môn thì sao?") is answered within the previous topic only when a clause of the previously cited article itself states that condition. Otherwise it is answered on its own, with a visible note that no clause links it to the previous question.

**Why:** the tester expected the assistant to connect such a question to the preceding scholarship answer, but the real corpus has no rule linking retakes to scholarships; inventing the link would violate the grounding contract, while silently answering a different question looked like lost context.

**How to apply:** never lower evidence floors for this case and never let history supply evidence. Abstaining on "does X affect Y?" when no clause states it is correct behaviour, not a bug.
